KOT Format — Current vs Petpooja Style

Both are for 80mm paper (48 chars). Click the one you prefer.

CURRENT FORMAT
KOT ================================================ Table: 4 Ord: 001234 25 May 2026 06:30 PM ------------------------------------------------ Sl.No Item Name Qty. ------------------------------------------------ 1 Butter Naan 2 2 Dal Makhani 1 3 Chicken Biryani Large 1 ------------------------------------------------ Total Items : 3

Plain, small header. No emphasis on table number. Hard to read quickly in a busy kitchen.

PETPOOJA STYLE ✦ RECOMMENDED
TABLE - 4
KITCHEN ORDER
================================================
KOT#: 001        25 May 2026       06:30 PM
------------------------------------------------
[ 02 ]  Butter Naan
        >> No Onion, Less Spice
[ 01 ]  Dal Makhani
[ 01 ]  Chicken Biryani Large
        >> Extra Rice
================================================
              Total Items: 4
================================================

Table number extra-large at top. Quantities in [ 02 ] brackets — chefs can't miss the count. Modifiers indented below.

Key differences in Petpooja KOT:

  • Table number in double-size bold (ESC/POS double-height + double-width)
  • Quantities in [ 02 ] format — easy to read at a glance
  • Modifiers/instructions printed with >> indent below item
  • Clean ==== borders top and bottom
  • Takeaway orders show TAKEAWAY in the large header instead
